The Learning Notebook · included free

How do we keep AI safe? Ask a robot dog.

A picture-book guide to the big rules grown-ups use so AI doesn't cause trouble. Written for curious kids — and, it turns out, the clearest explanation many professionals have ever read. Same four rules, no jargon. If you can understand a robot dog, you already understand the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

🐕 Imagine you got a robot dog for your birthday. It can fetch your slippers, do tricks, and even talk! But… what if it doesn't understand "sit", or knocks over grandma's vase, or shares your secrets with the neighbour's robot?

That "what could go wrong" feeling is called risk. Grown-ups built a set of rules to help people make AI — and robot dogs — safely. Here are the 4 big rules 👇
🏛️
GOVERN

Make the family rules before you even get the robot dog.

"No robot dogs near the pool" — decided ahead of time.
🗺️
MAP

Figure out what it will actually be used for, and who might be affected.

It fetches slippers in the living room — not babysit the baby!
📏
MEASURE

Test it! See how often it messes up, and write it down.

It fetched slippers correctly 9 out of 10 times.
🛠️
MANAGE

Fix the problems you find — or turn it off if it's too risky.

It keeps knocking the vase, so move the vase or retrain it.

🔁 You don't do this once — you loop through all 4, forever, as long as you use the AI.

🚦 The traffic-light rule book (Europe's AI law)

🚫
BannedNot allowed at all — like AI that spies on everyone's behaviour to score them.

High RiskBig rules to follow — like AI deciding who gets hired or a loan.
💬
Just Be HonestMust tell you it's AI — like a chatbot or a deepfake video.
✅
No Big DealTotally fine — like a spam filter or a video game character.

The rulebooks, side by side

NIST, ISO 42001 and the EU AI Act — in one table.

This is the crosswalk we teach on Day 7, and the single most useful page in the programme. Read it once and you'll never again confuse a voluntary framework with binding law. One control framework can satisfy all three — you just have to map it once.

ConcernNIST AI RMFISO/IEC 42001EU AI Act
What it isVoluntary risk-management guidance (US)Certifiable management-system standard (global)Binding law (EU market)
Core structure4 functions: Govern, Map, Measure, ManageManagement-system clauses (context, leadership, planning, support, operation, evaluation, improvement) + Annex A controlsRisk tiers: unacceptable, high-risk, limited-risk, minimal-risk
Governance & oversightGovern function — policies, accountability, cultureClause 5 (Leadership) + Annex A.2 (AI policy)Conformity assessment + human oversight obligations (Art. 14) for high-risk systems
Risk identificationMap function — context, use case, impactClause 6 (Planning) — risk & impact assessmentRisk-tier classification (Annex III lists high-risk use cases)
Risk measurementMeasure function — testing, metrics, bias/robustnessAnnex A.6 (data), A.7 (resources), performance monitoringTechnical documentation, conformity assessment, testing (Art. 9–15)
Ongoing managementManage function — response, monitoring, improvementClauses 9–10 (Evaluation, Improvement) — internal audit, corrective actionPost-market monitoring (Art. 72), incident reporting
Certifiable?No — self-assessed frameworkYes — third-party certification availableN/A — legal compliance, enforced by regulators and fines
Best used forInternal maturity model, common vocabularyBuilding an auditable AI management systemDetermining legal obligations for EU-market AI systems
🗣️

NIST gives the vocabulary

Four functions — Govern, Map, Measure, Manage — that everyone in the room can share, in any sector, in any country.

🏗️

ISO gives the system

An auditable management system with clauses and controls, so good intentions become evidence a third party can check.

⚖️

The AI Act gives the tiers

Legal risk tiers that decide how hard you apply the other two. High-risk EU system: full weight. Minimal-risk internal tool: lightweight.

"A high-risk EU system needs the full weight of both frameworks. A minimal-risk internal tool may only need lightweight NIST-style governance. Knowing which is which is the job."

Straight answers

Frequently asked.

Do I need a technical or legal background?

No. The programme is written in plain language for professionals of every kind — business, admin, tech, data, audit, students and career-changers. If you can read a work email, you can do this.

Really only $29? What's the catch?

No catch, no tiers, no upsell wall. $29 gives you the full programme, the games, the certificate and lifetime updates. We price it this way because we want governance literacy to spread widely, not stay elite.

How much time per day?

Around 10–20 minutes. Ten short lessons designed around how memory actually works: a concept, a story, a game, a recall test. You can also sprint it in two days if you prefer.

Is the certificate recognised?

You create and own the certificate through the programme. It is a verifiable record of completing a structured 10-day AI governance curriculum — useful for LinkedIn, internal promotion cases and client credibility.

Which standards and laws do you cover?

EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, OECD AI Principles and the major national frameworks, translated into practical decisions rather than legal theory. We update them as rules change.

Is this about controlling AI, or banning it?

Controlling it — with humans firmly in charge. You'll learn how to set boundaries, evidence decisions, catch bias and risk early, and keep AI serving people rather than the reverse.

Is the Learning Notebook (the robot dog) included?

Yes — and it is free to share. It is the friendliest explanation of NIST's four functions and the EU AI Act's risk tiers you will find anywhere. Many people forward it to their team, their kids or their boss before they decide anything else.

Do you prepare me for an external certification like the IAPP AIGP?

No — and we want to be clear about that. The AIGP is a certification run by IAPP; we are not a training partner and we do not sell, teach or prepare for their exam. What we do is teach the underlying frameworks in plain language and give you our own AI Governance Expert certificate for completing the 10-day programme. If you later choose to sit an external exam you will find the concepts familiar, but that is a separate path on their terms.
